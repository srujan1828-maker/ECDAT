# ECDAT V4 P2.3 — Blast Radius Explainability & Audit Reports

## 1. Explainability Philosophy
Security engineers and audit bodies require transparent, machine-verifiable justification for why an asset has a given blast radius. Black-box metrics or ungrounded percentages are prohibited by ECDAT V4.

Every assessment produces a step-by-step `reason_chain` composed of `BlastRadiusReason` objects:
- `step`: Lifecycle stage of evaluation.
- `claim`: Clear human-readable deduction.
- `rule_id`: Deterministic knowledge base rule (e.g., `BLST-PTH-001`, `BLST-CRT-001`).
- `rule_version`: Version of rule applied (`1.0`).
- `supporting`: Specific evidence items and observed facts.
- `contradicting`: Conflicting or disconfirming indicators.
- `unknowns`: Unobserved attributes.
- `limitations`: Boundaries triggered (e.g., depth limit, cycle pruning).

---

## 2. Structured Reason Steps
1. `DIRECT_DEPENDENCY_IDENTIFICATION`: Reports observed immediate consumers and callers directly attached to the target.
2. `TRANSITIVE_PROPAGATION_ANALYSIS`: Reports multi-hop propagation chains through intermediate libraries, wrappers, or microservices.
3. `IMPACT_SCOPE_CLASSIFICATION`: Explains the architectural boundary category (`LOCAL`, `MULTI_SERVICE`, `CROSS_DEPLOYMENT`, `WIDESPREAD`).
4. `ARCHITECTURAL_CRITICALITY_EVALUATION`: Details why criticality is `CRITICAL`, `HIGH`, `MEDIUM`, or `LOW` based on concrete deployment and data protection attributes.

---
